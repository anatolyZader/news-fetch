## Executive Summary

Every one of the six recurring findings is a **tooling artifact, and every one is already fixed
in-tree** — the recurrence is frozen into old renders, not live. Five of the six are the PBO officer
score-line template (`[אעבלין] רציפות תפקודית: avg=50% (0%, 100%) — …`) promoted verbatim into claim
text; the sixth is the critique tool itself mislabelling a legitimate, embedded open-observation ref
as external. All six judges returned `artifact` at high confidence, each having opened the underlying
reports and resolved the cited refs.

The decisive evidence is a date boundary, verified independently of the judges: `stripFieldReportScoreBlob`
landed 2026-07-24 and `isScoreLineOnlyClaim` / `cleanClaimText` landed 2026-08-08, while the two reports
carrying all five score-line findings were rendered 2026-06-12 and 2026-06-14. Counting `avg=NN%`
occurrences across renders shows the cut exactly: 1,534 in the 2026-07-24T13:15Z render, **0** in the
2026-07-24T15:17Z render, and 0 in every render since — including the 2026-08-14 report audited by the
companion `/review-signals` pass.

**So this run's highest-leverage action is not an analysis fix — it is to stop re-litigating settled
history.** The single change worth making is to the critique tool: teach it to resolve `open:` refs
against the report's own `_evidence_graph`, and give it a `--produced-after` default so pre-epoch
renders stop dominating the recurrence ranking. Until then this pass will keep spending judges on
defects that were repaired weeks ago.

What this run did **not** examine deserves equal emphasis: 17 claims are flagged unsupported, but none
of them recurs across two or more reports, so none crossed the `--min-recurrence 2` threshold and
**none was judged**. They are the open question, not a clean bill of health.

- Four of eight reports contributed no claims at all, so 177 claims come from four reports, and two of

### Findings 1–5 — the PBO score-line template, already fixed

`pbo-אעבלין` signal in both reports, with the same frozen `story_cluster_id` on both dates
(e.g. `cluster:pbo:solidarity_help_others:dec75027…`) despite different source bundles
(`north_11_4.xlsx` vs `north_17_4.xlsx`). The officer's score-form header was echoed straight through
as an analytical claim. Nothing about the field changed between the dates; the template recurred.

Note how empty the underlying answers are once the score header is removed: `stripFieldReportScoreBlob`
reduces finding 1/3 to `[אעבלין] אין` — literally "[Ibillin] none" — which
`isTrivialFieldReportEvidence` then drops as boilerplate. Findings 2/4 reduce to
`[אעבלין] מכלול אוכלוסיה בתפקוד מלא` and finding 5 to `[אעבלין] רשות בתפקוד מלא`.

### Finding 0 — the critique tool's own ref resolver

`crossReportCritique.js:37` defines `EXTERNAL_REF` as a ref that "points outside the signal namespace
… **and is not embedded in the report**", but it never checks the second half. `claimRefNamespace.js:30`
declares `open:obs-N` a first-class namespace and `:126` provides an `openObservationsByRef` store for
resolving it, which the critique pass does not supply.

The observation is in fact embedded. In `north-1-data-2026-04-09-produced-2026-06-22T0918Z.json`,
`assessment._evidence_graph.nodes.signals` contains:

```json
{ "id": "open:obs-2", "signal_type": "open_observation", "source_type": "field",
  "evidence": "Young people fled the community", "grounding_tier": "unverified" }
```

The claim text is the evidence text, verbatim. So `external_ref` with `support_count: 0` is the
critique tool failing to look in `_evidence_graph`, not a claim asserting more than it can carry.

**One honest qualification:** the observation's own `grounding_tier` is `unverified`, and the report
already tags it `epistemic_flags: ["unverified", "open_observation"]` and raises an
`open_observation_unaddressed` warning. The claim is fully *traceable*; it simply rests on unverified
field material, and the pipeline says so. Fixing the resolver will move this claim from a false
`external_ref` to a correctly-labelled unverified-support claim — not to clean.

## Cross-Finding Patterns

- **One municipality drives the entire recurrence set.** `pbo-אעבלין` accounts for 23 of the weighted
  source weaknesses — nearly three times the next source — and supplies five of six findings. This is a
  single input template repeating, not a pattern across the field.
- **Two reports supply five findings.** 2026-04-11 and 2026-04-17, rendered two days apart in June from
  the same pre-fix code. Recurrence here measures render count, not field persistence.
- **All six findings are `verbatim_repeat: true`** while the underlying situation demonstrably changed
  (different source workbooks, different dates). That is the signature of a template, and the judges
  used it correctly.
- **The weak signal types overlap the confusion pairs just re-scoped in catalog v10.**
  `signal_type_weakness` is led by `community_volunteering` (12), `service_continuity` (9),
  `solidarity_help_others` (8) and `wellbeing_support_accessed` (7) — three of those four are exactly

  state-from-act. Worth re-running this pass after a post-v10 report exists, to see whether their
  weakness share moves.

## Improvement Proposals

1. **Resolve `open:` refs against the report's own evidence graph** — `crossReportCritique.js` should
   pass an `openObservationsByRef` store built from `assessment._evidence_graph.nodes.signals`
   (and/or `open_observations_summary`) into the resolver at `claimRefNamespace.js:126`, and reserve
   `EXTERNAL_REF` for refs genuinely absent from the report, as its own doc comment at `:37` already
   specifies. Evidence: finding 0. Expected impact: removes a whole class of false `external_ref` /
   `support_count: 0` findings; the affected claims re-surface correctly as unverified-support rather
   than untraceable.

2. **Default the critique window to one render epoch** — add a `--produced-after` default (or warn and
   exclude) so renders predating the newest catalog/hygiene epoch do not dominate the recurrence
   ranking. This run spent all six judges on defects repaired between 2026-07-24 and 2026-08-08.
   Evidence: findings 1–5 plus the `render_epoch_spread` caveat. Expected impact: the pass starts
   reporting live defects instead of settled history.

3. **Raise `--min-recurrence` findings above the artifact floor, or judge the singletons** — 17
   unsupported claims exist and none was examined, because none recurred. Either lower the bar for
   `unsupported`-tier claims specifically (they are the tier that matters) or run a separate singleton
   pass over them. Evidence: `totals.unsupported_claims: 17` against 0 unsupported findings. Expected
   impact: the pass stops reporting "no recurring unsupported claims" as though it meant "no
   unsupported claims".

4. **Treat `empty_assessment` as a run failure, not a caveat line** — 2026-04-12 produced zero claims
   off 782 scoped signals and 60 articles, and 3 more reports were empty-thin, so half the corpus
   contributed nothing. This is the known silent-empty-assessment condition. Evidence:
   `reports_without_claims: 4`. Expected impact: a critique run over a half-empty corpus stops looking
   like a critique run over eight reports.

5. **Re-run this pass once a post-v10 report exists** — the weak signal types are dominated by three of
   the four confusion pairs re-scoped on 2026-08-15. Evidence: `signal_type_weakness` head. Expected
   impact: measures whether the catalog label rewrite actually moved claim quality, which nothing
   currently tests.
