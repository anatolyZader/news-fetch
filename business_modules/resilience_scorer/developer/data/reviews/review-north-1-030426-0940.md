# Signal Review: north-1-030426-0940
Date: 2026-04-03 | Scope: north | Signals: 415 | Articles: 57 | Window: 1 day (2026-04-03)

Sources: `north_3_4.xlsx` (PBO forms, 49 municipalities), `articles-visits-reports-2026-03-24.md` + `-2026-03-29.md` (field visits), `articles-homefront-2026-04-03.md` (news).

## Executive Summary

This report is not an extraction — for 85% of its evidence it is a **form transcription**. All 353 PBO signals come from a fixed 8-question municipal questionnaire that maps 1:1 onto the 8 resilience components: 31 of 49 municipalities produced the *identical* 8-signal type set, and no municipality ever produced two signals of the same type. `signal_count` therefore measures *how many form fields were filled in*, not how much of a phenomenon was observed. Every one of those 353 signals is stamped `pbo_completeness: incomplete` and `pbo_evidence_thin: true`, and the resilience scorer reads neither flag — those fields are written by `pbo_report_review` and consumed nowhere in scoring (`business_modules/pbo_report_review/domain/services/reviewSupplementalTexts.js:44-59`).

The second-order effect is a systematic positivity bias. The form's per-question extractor picks the mirror pair's *positive* member almost unconditionally: 45 `resilience_narrative_positive` vs 1 negative, 44 `compliance_enter_shelter` vs 2 non-compliance, 40 `service_continuity` vs 4 disruption, 44 `wellbeing_support_accessed` vs 1 distress. Free-text answers that plainly state the negative are still filed positive — בית ג'ן's *"residents continue in a state of uncertainty and worry… my fear is that the prolonged fighting will lead to complacency"* is filed as `resilience_narrative_positive`; גולן's *"the authority provides services… **but** the lack of schooling affects parents' work"* is filed as `service_continuity` with no disruption counterpart.

Weighting makes the imbalance worse than the counts suggest. PBO signals carry `temporal_weight: 1.0`; the 59 visits signals are all dated 2026-03-24 and carry ~0.30 after decay. Weighted mass is roughly **PBO 353 / visits 18 / news 3 — 94% PBO**. Yet the visits are the only source producing granular, differentiated, falsifiable evidence (healthcare service damage by service line, mamads stuck in construction for years, welfare-worker burnout since 2020, zero protection time). The report's sharpest evidence is its most discounted.

Most actionable finding: **`information_communication` is entirely unevidenced.** All 45 of its signals are PBO, and its top contributors are channel inventories — *"WhatsApp groups and national media, radio and TV"*, *"social media"*, *"HFC, authority, media"* — filed as `information_actionable_effective`. The component's guiding questions ask about effectiveness, gaps, misinformation, and accessibility to vulnerable groups. Not one of those is addressed by a channel list. The component's `narrative_grounding_score` is **0**.

## Cross-Cutting Patterns

**Form-template artifact (the dominant finding).** 353 of 415 signals come from a 49-municipality questionnaire with exactly 8 questions, one per component. 31 municipalities produced the identical type set; no municipality ever produced two signals of the same type. Per-question `article_index` grouping confirms a strict ≤1-signal-per-type-per-form structure. Consequences: (a) `signal_count` is a survey-response count, not an observation count; (b) a municipality with a rich three-paragraph answer and one with a two-word answer contribute identically; (c) all cross-municipality variance in the corpus is invisible to the scorer.

**Every PBO signal is self-flagged incomplete.** All 353 carry `pbo_completeness: "incomplete"` and `pbo_evidence_thin: true` (set when the municipal review is neither `sufficient` nor `resolved`). `grep` finds consumers only inside `business_modules/pbo_report_review/` — the resilience scorer ignores both fields. Median PBO evidence length is 133 chars; 21 signals are under 40 chars ("HFC, authority, media"; "perceived as credible"; "full-function authority").

**Positivity bias in mirror selection.** Positive:negative ratios within mirror pairs — narrative 45:1, compliance 44:2, continuity 40:4, volunteering 37:3, wellbeing 44:1. The free text does not support this: at least four sampled answers state explicit negatives (בית ג'ן narrative, גולן continuity, בוקעאתא 70% compliance, בית ג'ן youth non-compliance) that were filed positive or truncated.

**Over-used types.** `wellbeing_support_accessed` 52×, `community_volunteering` 50×, `leadership_visible_presence` 48×, `resilience_narrative_positive` 47×, `information_actionable_effective` 45×, `compliance_enter_shelter` 44×, `solidarity_help_others` 41×, `service_continuity` 40×. Each is a form-question default. The two most stretched are `information_actionable_effective` (channel inventories) and `leadership_visible_presence` (any authority communication act).

**Inferred-edge inflation.** `functional_continuity` 60 of 61 contexts inferred (53 positive) — mostly `wellbeing_support_accessed → inferred +`. `leadership` 47 of 53 inferred (43 positive) — `compliance_enter_shelter → inferred +`. `belonging_solidarity` 43 inferred, **all positive, zero negative** — same compliance edge. Three of the four "high confidence" components rest primarily on inferred rather than primary routing.

**Source concentration.** PBO is 85% by count and, after temporal weighting, **~94% by mass** (PBO `temporal_weight` 1.0 × 353; visits ~0.30 × 59 ≈ 18; news 1.0 × 3). Every component carries a `concentration_warning` (pbo 0.70–0.98, except wellbeing where it is visits 0.91). Quality runs opposite to weight: visits evidence is specific and falsifiable, PBO evidence is thin and templated, and visits are discounted 3.3×.

**Stale field evidence.** All 59 visits signals are dated 2026-03-24 — 10 days before the report date, in a 1-day assessment window. The sole granular negative evidence in the report (healthcare service damage, mamads stuck in construction, welfare burnout since 2020, zero protection time) is a week and a half old and decayed to ~0.30.

## Improvement Proposals

1. **Stop treating a filled form field as an observation — gate PBO signals on evidence substance.** Add a pre-routing filter that drops or demotes PBO signals whose evidence is a bare list or under a length/content threshold (21 signals are <40 chars; six of `information_communication`'s top ten are channel inventories). Concretely: require `information_actionable_effective` to contain a statement about reach, uptake, clarity, or gaps — not merely a channel name — and emit a non-scoring `channel_inventory` capture otherwise. *Expected impact:* `information_communication` (removes ~60% of its mass and its false "medium confidence"), `lifesaving_behavior` (removes 45 unearned primary + edges).

2. **Make the extractor emit multiple signals per form answer, including the negative half.** The single largest quality loss is one-signal-per-question truncation: בוקעאתא's 70% compliance, בית ג'ן's non-compliant youth and uncertainty narrative, גולן's schooling-driven work disruption, כפר מנדא's "deepening social gaps" were all discarded because the answer had already produced its positive signal. Remove the per-question cap and instruct the extractor to emit a mirror signal whenever an answer contains an adversative ("אך", "אבל", "לצד", "עם זאת"). *Expected impact:* all eight components; corrects the 45:1 / 44:2 / 40:4 positivity ratios directly, and would likely flip `functional_continuity` and `narrative` readings.

3. **Consume `pbo_evidence_thin` / `pbo_completeness` in scoring confidence.** These flags are written on every PBO signal and read by nothing outside `pbo_report_review`. Feed them into the component confidence calculation so that a component whose evidence is 85% `incomplete`-flagged cannot report `confidence: high`. Four components currently report `high` while carrying `assessment_state: assessed_low_confidence` — the two fields already disagree. *Expected impact:* `community_capital`, `leadership`, `belonging_solidarity`, `functional_continuity` — all four "high confidence" readings rest on thin-flagged evidence.

4. **Surface the wellbeing counter-evidence instead of rendering `0 positive / 11 negative`.** Keep the v7 routing rule, but have the `wellbeing_at_risk` component narrative state explicitly that N municipalities reported active at-risk response routed to `community_capital` by design. As rendered, the component reads as a collapse when 44 of 49 municipalities described a functioning welfare response. *Expected impact:* `wellbeing_at_risk` — removes a misleading one-sided-negative headline without touching the catalog.

5. **Cap or discount inferred-edge mass per component.** `functional_continuity` (60/61 inferred), `leadership` (47/53), and `belonging_solidarity` (43 inferred, all positive) are majority-inferred. When inferred contexts exceed primary ones, the component should not be eligible for `confidence: high`, and the top-contributor display should mark inferred contributors. The compliance→belonging inferred edge in particular is reading shelter behavior as social cohesion 44 times. *Expected impact:* `belonging_solidarity`, `leadership`, `functional_continuity`.

6. **Add a volunteer-capacity-gap type and fix three severity overreaches.** Register a negative mirror for `community_volunteering` (volunteer array absent / no coordinator / attrition) so יבנאל, מגדל and מעלות תרשיחא stop being filed as `dependency_on_external_aid` against the catalog's own reject pattern. Separately: tighten `early_warning_system_failure` to exclude geographic zero-warning-time, and `public_order_breakdown` to exclude loitering. *Expected impact:* `community_capital` (correct negatives instead of wrong ones), `lifesaving_behavior`, `information_communication`.

7. **Fix visits dedup and same-report contradiction detection.** `_semantic_dedup_count` is 1 for all 415 signals despite four near-identical healthcare-disruption entries from two visitor pairs inflating `functional_continuity`'s top-10. Add a same-source/same-type similarity collapse, and flag the case where one visit report yields both `coordination_failure` and `leadership_visible_presence` about the same HFC"I team. *Expected impact:* `functional_continuity`, `leadership`.
