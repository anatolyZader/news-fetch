Why it earns trust

  • Evidence you can open — narratives trace to raw excerpts under each component
  • Ask the reports — chat answers from cited evidence, not the open internet
  • No single number as the hero — read narrative and evidence, not a headline score
  • Recognized framework — shared language with HQ and field

The field completes the picture

PBO regional reports, the WhatsApp report bot, and manual submissions feed the daily assessment — including the same day’s run. Field judgment stays primary.

What is it? Evidence-backed daily resilience assessment across eight components; humans decide.

Does it predict? No — it assesses today’s observable picture from signals in text.

Why no single score? Eight dimensions stay visible; one number would hide what is behind it.
