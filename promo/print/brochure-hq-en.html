Attention is the scarce resource

Structure the signal. Show the evidence. Let humans decide.

In a homefront crisis, signal is scattered across news, radio, social media, field reports, and search behavior. The temptation is to compress it into a single number that hides what is behind it.

srulik.ai produces one assessment a day across eight Home Front Command community-resilience components. Every narrative links to raw excerpts — not a headline score.

Every claim traceable

Inline citations and an evidence pool under each component.

Ask the reports

Chat answers for today and past dates — same cited evidence.

Field + digital, fused

PBO, WhatsApp bot, and submissions in one daily run.

Recognized framework

Shared language with HQ and field — Pikud HaOref model.

How it works

  1. Extract observable behavioral signals from multi-source text
  2. Verify the evidence behind each signal
  3. Assess eight community-resilience components
  4. Narrate user prose with inline source citations

Data sources include news, WhatsApp, radio/audio, Google Trends, and user submissions.

The field completes the picture

PBO reports, WhatsApp bot, and manual submissions feed the same-day assessment. Field judgment stays primary.

Three ways to contribute

  1. Regional report (PBO) — upload markdown; enters evidence pool
  2. WhatsApp report bot — short observation becomes a signal
  3. Manual submission — text or document in the app

Your observation → signal in today’s run, not held for tomorrow.
