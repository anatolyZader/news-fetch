## Srulik's lab
### Community resilience · Daily assessment

**Evidence-backed daily homefront resilience assessment from news, social media, field reports, and manual submissions.**

---

### The problem

In a homefront crisis, attention is the scarce resource. Signal is scattered across news, radio, social media, field reports, and search behavior — and the temptation is to compress it into a single number that hides what's behind it.

### The approach

srulik.ai produces **one daily assessment** across eight community-resilience components, drawn from multiple sources, where **every claim links to the evidence behind it**. It structures the picture; humans decide.

> **Not an oracle.** It structures the picture; humans decide.

### How it works

1. **Extract** observable behavioral signals from multi-source text (closed vocabulary).
2. **Verify** the evidence behind each signal.
3. **Assess** the eight components.
4. **Narrate** operator-safe prose with inline `[source]` citations.

### The eight components

Narrative · Information & Communication · Effective Life-Saving Behavior · Functional Continuity · Community Capital · Leadership · Belonging & Solidarity · Wellbeing of At-Risk Populations
*(Home Front Command / Pikud HaOref community-resilience framework.)*

### Why it earns trust

- **Evidence you can open** — every component narrative traces to raw excerpts; an evidence pool sits under each one.
- **Ask the reports** — chat answers questions for today and across past reports (compare dates, trace a component over time) and cites the same evidence, not the open internet.
- **No single number** — operators read narrative and evidence, not a headline score.
- **A recognized framework** — the eight components are the Home Front Command model: a shared language with HQ and field, not a bespoke metric.

### For the field

PBO regional reports, a WhatsApp report bot, and manual submissions feed the daily assessment — including the same day's run. The field completes the picture; field judgment stays primary.

### Scope & languages

National and North · English, Hebrew, Russian · opt-in daily email digest.

---

**What it is not:** not a news aggregator, not a resilience-score app, not a prediction engine, not a replacement for field officers.
