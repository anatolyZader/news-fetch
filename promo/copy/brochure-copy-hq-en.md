## Page 2 — Why + How (max ~220 words)

### Headline: Attention is the scarce resource

In a homefront crisis, signal is scattered across news, radio, social media, field reports, and search behavior. The temptation is to compress it into a single number that hides what is behind it.

### Subhead: One daily assessment — every claim openable

srulik.ai structures one daily read across eight components. Every narrative links to the evidence behind it. **Decision-support, not an oracle — humans decide.**

### Pipeline (visual)

1. **Extract** — observable behavioral signals from multi-source text  
2. **Verify** — evidence behind each signal  
3. **Assess** — eight community-resilience components  
4. **Narrate** — operator prose with inline source citations  

### Scope line

National and North · English, Hebrew, Russian · opt-in daily email digest

## Page 4 — Back (max ~200 words)

### Why it earns trust

- **Evidence you can open** — narratives trace to raw excerpts under each component  
- **Ask the reports** — chat answers from cited evidence, not the open internet  
- **No single number as the hero** — read narrative and evidence, not a headline score  
- **Recognized framework** — shared language with HQ and field  

### The field completes the picture

PBO regional reports, the WhatsApp report bot, and manual submissions feed the daily assessment — including the same day’s run. Field judgment stays primary.

### FAQ (short)

**What is it?** Evidence-backed daily resilience assessment across eight components; humans decide.  
**Does it predict?** No — it assesses today’s observable picture from signals in text.  
**Why no single score?** Eight dimensions stay visible; one number would hide what is behind it.
