# Canonical Demo Script — srulik.ai (8 minutes)

> One recorded demo to rule them all. Record once with OBS/Loom at **https://srulik.ai** on a **good-assessment day** (not an empty state), with a **visible assessment date**. Capture EN; re-record HE for field audiences. Keep total runtime 8–9 min. Chapter markers below double as video chapters and as the SRT caption skeleton.
>
> **Scope:** user-facing core only — daily assessment, open evidence, chat, multi-source, field contribution. Do not show internal scoring instruments or the developer view (see `do-not-say.md`).
>
> **Tone:** calm, professional, no hype. Lead with evidence, close with "humans decide." Never say "predicts," never lead with a number.

---

## Pre-flight checklist

- [ ] A day loaded with real signal across several components (one clearly "hot").
- [ ] Assessment date visible in the footer (`footer.assessmentDate`).
- [ ] Logged-in or default user view (not developer).
- [ ] Have one component picked in advance that has a rich evidence pool.
- [ ] Browser zoom set so text is legible at 1080p.

---

## Chapter 1 — The daily picture (0:00–1:30)

**Screen:** Home — daily assessment, eight components.

> "This is srulik.ai. Every day it produces one homefront resilience assessment across eight community-resilience components — the Home Front Command framework: narrative, information, life-saving behavior, functional continuity, community capital, leadership, belonging, and the wellbeing of at-risk populations."

- Point to the **assessment date**. "Everything you see is grounded in a specific day's evidence."
- "It's not a news feed and it's not a single score. It's a structured daily read of how the community is holding."

## Chapter 2 — Open a component (1:30–3:30)

**Screen:** Expand one **hot** component → narrative + evidence pool.

> "Let's open the component that's most active today."

- Read one sentence of the narrative aloud.
- Scroll to the **evidence pool**. "Every claim in that narrative is backed by raw excerpts — news, radio, a WhatsApp report, a field submission. Click any citation and you land on the source."
- Click one inline `[source]` citation. "Nothing here is the model's opinion floating free — it traces back."
- "And there's no single headline number doing the talking — you read the narrative and you check the evidence."

## Chapter 3 — Chat: interrogate the report (3:30–5:00)

**Screen:** Chat on today's report.

> "You don't have to read everything. Ask the report."

- Type a real question, e.g. **"What should I verify in the field today?"**, "Why is functional continuity flagged?", or "How did this component change over the past week?"
- "Chat answers across the reports — today's and past — so you can compare dates or trace a component's evolution, and every answer cites the same evidence, not the open internet."
- Click through one cited source from the chat answer. "Same discipline as the report: every answer is openable."

## Chapter 4 — The field completes the picture (5:00–7:00)

**Screen:** Data-sources bar → then submissions / report-bot / PBO regional reports.

> "Where does the evidence come from? News, WhatsApp, radio, Google Trends, and submissions from people on the ground."

- Open the **data-sources** view; trace one signal back to its origin (news / radio / PBO).
- Show the **submission / report-bot** flow. "A PBO uploads a regional report, or someone sends the WhatsApp bot a note — that becomes a signal in the daily assessment, and same-day field reports are picked up by that day's run, not held for tomorrow. The field isn't a spectator; it's an input."

## Chapter 5 — Close (7:00–8:00)

**Screen:** Back to home / footer with tagline.

> "So: a daily, multi-source assessment across eight components; every claim you can open; ask the report what you need; and the field keeps it grounded. It structures the picture and shows the evidence — **humans decide.**"

- **CTA:** "Read today's assessment at srulik.ai. If you're in the field, send a report — it makes the picture sharper."
