## Purpose
Srulik's lab is a **decision-support system** for homefront operators. It helps you see evidence and uncertainty quickly—you remain responsible for judgment and action.

## Recommended workflow
1. Read [Using the app (first report)](using-the-app.md) for the two-pass read (scan, then proof).
2. Follow [Operator workflow](../guides/operator-workflow.md) for a daily routine.
3. Read [When not to act](../guides/when-not-to-act.md) before escalating on thin evidence.

## Troubleshooting
- **The report feels incomplete**
  - **Check**: evidence overview counts and stale-date banner.
  - **Fix**: submit evidence or ask your operator to run today's pipeline; abstention may be intentional.
- **I want the full mental model**
  - **Fix**: open [Decision support model](../concepts/decision-support-model.md) on [full docs](https://docs.srulik.ai/).
