## Executive Summary

Today's signal base derives from a single reported source — a video report documenting shelter conditions in deteriorating residential neighborhoods — which constrains the breadth of behavioral observation available. Within that constrained sample, the dominant behavioral pattern is one of residents actively improvising protective solutions in the absence of adequate infrastructure: families relocating to stairwells, underground parking garages, or substandard shelters at night, while caregivers rotate shifts managing dependent elderly relatives through repeated alert cycles. Compliance with protective guidance is present and consistent, but it operates against a backdrop of structural deficit rather than institutional support, with more than half of Israeli residents reported to lack a protected space within their homes. The coping behavior documented is adaptive and self-organized rather than institutionally enabled.
