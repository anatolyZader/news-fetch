## Executive Summary

The dominant behavioral pattern on April 1 is a structural split between the visibility of institutional responsiveness and the uneven distribution of its benefits across population groups. Formal leadership channels are active and producing updated operational guidance — the IDF revised interception policy for northern highways on Passover eve, Home Front Command issued silent-alert reminders, and multiple government portals publicized emergency support services. Shelter compliance is broadly documented in central and northern communities, and consumer spending data indicates that household Passover preparations proceeded at or above prior-year levels in the center of the country. However, these national-level functioning indicators coexist with documented structural deficits concentrated in specific populations: Arab communities report persistent shelter shortages with no state remediation, the West Bank fuel supply is under sustained financial pressure, and Christian minority communities describe an accumulating pattern of violence and emigration that represents a longer-term erosion of belonging. The Passover holiday context amplified the narrative of collective resilience in official and media messaging, while simultaneously making visible the populations for whom that narrative does not apply.

## Components

**What each column means**

- **Assessment reliability** — How much we trust the **component score** (low / medium / high), based on both how strong the weighted evidence is and how broadly it appears across articles.
- **Evidence level** — A **0–100% certainty** score: how much *weighted* behavioral evidence we found for this component. **It is not “how good resilience is.”** High % means we had enough signal strength to be confident in the scoring step; the **Component score** (1–10) tells you favorable vs unfavorable.
- **Evidence base** — Number of behavioral signals tagged to this component.
- **Article coverage** — How many *distinct* articles in today’s sample contributed at least one signal, as a fraction of all articles analyzed.

**How Evidence level % is calculated (technical)** — Each signal adds `|mapping weight| × scope weight × reliability weight`. Those add up to *evidence mass*. Certainty = `1 − e^(−evidence_mass / 2)`, shown as %. It saturates toward 100% as mass grows. **Direction** (supporting vs opposing) is separate and shown under Evidence direction.
