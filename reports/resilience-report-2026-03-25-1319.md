## Executive Summary

On March 25, the dominant behavioral pattern continues the trajectory established over prior days: physical protective compliance remains high and well-evidenced across geography, while the functional systems that sustain daily life remain at low capacity. Shelter use is directly linked to casualty prevention in multiple documented cases — a babysitter sheltering with two children in Petah Tikva, residents in Nesher whose building took a direct hit, a Kiryat Shmona family who moved their child before the siren — and this protective behavior is sustained at the population level. At the same time, the education system has now accumulated three days of contradictory ministry guidance, with daycare owners preparing to reopen only to be reversed within hours; the total economic cost of school closure is reported at approximately 1.25 billion shekels per week; and over 5,000 people have been hospitalized since the operation began on February 28. The gap between robust community solidarity and voluntarism on one side, and structurally unmet needs — 714,000 elderly and disabled without shelter access, a decade-old geriatric bed shortage, over three million residents without standard protected spaces — on the other, defines the central tension of this assessment period.

## Components

**What each column means**

- **Assessment reliability** — How much we trust the **component score** (low / medium / high), based on both how strong the weighted evidence is and how broadly it appears across articles.
- **Evidence level** — A **0–100% certainty** score: how much *weighted* behavioral evidence we found for this component. **It is not “how good resilience is.”** High % means we had enough signal strength to be confident in the scoring step; the **Component score** (1–10) tells you favorable vs unfavorable.
- **Evidence base** — Number of behavioral signals tagged to this component.
- **Article coverage** — How many *distinct* articles in today’s sample contributed at least one signal, as a fraction of all articles analyzed.

**How Evidence level % is calculated (technical)** — Each signal adds `|mapping weight| × scope weight × reliability weight`. Those add up to *evidence mass*. Certainty = `1 − e^(−evidence_mass / 2)`, shown as %. It saturates toward 100% as mass grows. **Direction** (supporting vs opposing) is separate and shown under Evidence direction.
