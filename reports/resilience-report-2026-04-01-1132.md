## Executive Summary

The dominant behavioral pattern on April 1 is a two-tier structure in which the narrative and leadership components perform at high levels while functional continuity and wellbeing for at-risk populations remain deeply compromised. Field reports covering more than forty visited communities across the Baram, Hiram, Maaleh Yosef, Matte Asher, and Upper Galilee regions document the same structural conditions repeated across settlements of different sizes and compositions: elderly residents isolated in homes they cannot safely leave, protection infrastructure absent or degraded in a majority of dwellings in several communities, educational and social frameworks closed or operating in reduced form, and HFC units functioning at partial capacity or without key personnel. Against this backdrop, a majority of residents are choosing to remain rather than evacuate, expressing coping narratives and community solidarity — a behavioral posture that is genuine but exists in tension with the physical infrastructure conditions documented by the same field reports. National-level consumer spending data showing a 5.5% increase in Passover eve transactions, and news-sourced signals of urban volunteer activity and leadership responsiveness, describe conditions in central population centers that differ structurally from the northern border communities forming the analytical core of this assessment.

## Components

**What each column means**

- **Assessment reliability** — How much we trust the **component score** (low / medium / high), based on both how strong the weighted evidence is and how broadly it appears across articles.
- **Evidence level** — A **0–100% certainty** score: how much *weighted* behavioral evidence we found for this component. **It is not “how good resilience is.”** High % means we had enough signal strength to be confident in the scoring step; the **Component score** (1–10) tells you favorable vs unfavorable.
- **Evidence base** — Number of behavioral signals tagged to this component.
- **Article coverage** — How many *distinct* articles in today’s sample contributed at least one signal, as a fraction of all articles analyzed.

**How Evidence level % is calculated (technical)** — Each signal adds `|mapping weight| × scope weight × reliability weight`. Those add up to *evidence mass*. Certainty = `1 − e^(−evidence_mass / 2)`, shown as %. It saturates toward 100% as mass grows. **Direction** (supporting vs opposing) is separate and shown under Evidence direction.
