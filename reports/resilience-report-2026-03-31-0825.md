## Executive Summary

On March 31, the dominant behavioral pattern remains one of sustained protective compliance paired with deepening structural strain — a pattern consistent with the prior two days, though several components show directional shifts. Lifesaving compliance and narrative cohesion remain high: residents across northern, central, and southern Israel continue entering shelters on alert, factory workers shelter in place following hazardous materials incidents, and public figures articulate the protective value of following Home Front Command instructions. Concurrently, a positive collective narrative circulates through military service stories, returned hostage voices, and shelter-based community-building. The structural split that most defines today's picture is between populations with adequate physical and informational access to protection — primarily urban, Hebrew-speaking, formally employed residents — and those without: Arab communities in the north lacking shelter infrastructure, returned hostages caught in open spaces during sirens, workers without legal protection to pause, and the 33% of the population the State Comptroller identified as lacking standard protection access.

## Components

**What each column means**

- **Assessment reliability** — How much we trust the **component score** (low / medium / high), based on both how strong the weighted evidence is and how broadly it appears across articles.
- **Evidence level** — A **0–100% certainty** score: how much *weighted* behavioral evidence we found for this component. **It is not “how good resilience is.”** High % means we had enough signal strength to be confident in the scoring step; the **Component score** (1–10) tells you favorable vs unfavorable.
- **Evidence base** — Number of behavioral signals tagged to this component.
- **Article coverage** — How many *distinct* articles in today’s sample contributed at least one signal, as a fraction of all articles analyzed.

**How Evidence level % is calculated (technical)** — Each signal adds `|mapping weight| × scope weight × reliability weight`. Those add up to *evidence mass*. Certainty = `1 − e^(−evidence_mass / 2)`, shown as %. It saturates toward 100% as mass grows. **Direction** (supporting vs opposing) is separate and shown under Evidence direction.
