## 5. kan-reka — מיקרופון פתוח כאן רקע 11:05-12:00 — scene 7: Expert explains pension fund investment portfolio options and age-based models

- **URL:** (audio recording)
- **Published:** 2026-04-09
- **Source:** kan-reka — מיקרופון פתוח כאן רקע 11:05-12:00

[Scene type: expert_interview | Speakers: financial expert (male), radio host]

A financial expert addresses pension fund investment strategy, explaining default investment allocations and the age-based (Glide Path) model introduced in 2016. He describes how investors are automatically assigned to risk-appropriate portfolios based on age brackets (under 50, 50-60, 60+), but warns that automatic transitions at fixed ages may lock in market losses. The expert recommends that engaged investors opt for manual control over portfolio transitions rather than autopilot systems, allowing them to time adjustments strategically. He also covers insurance options within pension funds, noting that default coverage for spouses and children may not suit all individuals, particularly young unmarried workers who could reduce costs by declining unnecessary coverage.

Key quotes:
- "If someone opens a pension program today and says nothing about how they want their investment portfolio, they are automatically assigned according to age."
- "I don't like this model because exactly at 50 and exactly at 60 they transfer you without notice, but at that moment there may be market declines, and the person catches the decline on a riskier portfolio, then gets moved to a less risky one."
- "For beginners, I'd recommend the default, but if someone starts taking some interest, the age-based portfolio without autopilot, without automatic transition to the next level."
- "Some people pay for insurance they don't need. Often it concerns young unmarried people who have no spouse and no children yet."

- **URL:** (audio recording)
- **Published:** 2026-04-09
- **Source:** kan-reka — מיקרופון פתוח כאן רקע 11:05-12:00

[Scene type: expert_interview | Speakers: financial expert (D), financial expert (A), financial expert (B), radio host]

The experts provide detailed procedural guidance on transferring between pension vehicles without interrupting contributions or losing coverage. They emphasize the critical sequence: first redirect employer contributions to the new pension fund, which then automatically pulls the accumulated savings from the old policy, rather than withdrawing first and then depositing. This approach preserves existing insurance coverage through the transition. The experts stress that maintaining continuity of coverage is especially important for older workers or those with health conditions, as reapplying for insurance later may be difficult or costly.

Key quotes:
- "The important thing to account for is not to interrupt—the transfer must first move the employer contributions, then these contributions draw to themselves the sum of money from the policy."
- "First the employer contributions, then this contribution pulls after it the accumulated savings."
- "To preserve any active insurance policies you want to keep in the transfer, especially if you're not 20 years old and your health is no longer that of a 20-year-old person."
