## What it is
Radio broadcasts, interviews, and audio clips **transcribed to text**, then analyzed like news and other evidence.

## In the app
Open **Data sources** → **Radio** (or **Audio**, depending on your deployment label). Read transcripts and metadata for what aired today.

## When to use it
- Follow official statements or recurring programs that rarely appear in print
- Cross-check a component claim against spoken sources
- Operators → [Audio ingestion](../../guides/audio-ingestion.md)

## Troubleshooting
- **Tab not visible** — radio ingestion may be disabled for your instance.
- **Transcript quality** — noisy audio can produce thin evidence; expand component evidence on the daily report to see confidence.
