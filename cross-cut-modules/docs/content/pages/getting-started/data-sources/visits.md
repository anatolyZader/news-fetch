## What it is
**Professional squad visits** to municipalities — structured field observations recorded after on-site work.

## In the app
Open **Data sources** → **Visits**. See what was recorded before trusting a score about a specific locality.

## When to use it
- Ground a component narrative in recent field presence
- Compare visit notes with PBO files and news for the same area

## Troubleshooting
- **No visits listed** — squads may not have visited that municipality this cycle.
- **Score still thin** — visits supplement other feeds; check evidence overview on the daily report.
