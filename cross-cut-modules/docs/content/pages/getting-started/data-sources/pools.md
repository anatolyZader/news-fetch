## What it is
Themed **longer-cycle feeds** under **Pools**:
- **Naftali** — weekly municipality welfare questionnaires
- **Education** — bi-weekly field sessions on schools

These inform components over **weeks**, not only today's headline news.

## In the app
Open **Data sources** → **Pools**. Switch between Naftali and Education dashboards inside the tab.

## When to use it
- Explain slow-moving component trends that daily news cannot capture
- Review questionnaire or session data before challenging a welfare or education-related score

## Troubleshooting
- **Data looks old** — pools update on weekly or bi-weekly cadence by design.
- **Missing pool** — your deployment may enable only one theme; ask your user.
