## What it is
**Public posts** and on-demand **topic searches** — citizen voice and OSINT-style collection. Not every deployment ingests all platforms.

## In the app
Open **Data sources** → **Social media**. Audit what was collected and which topics were queried.

## When to use it
- Sense-check grassroots sentiment not yet in news or WhatsApp
- Review collection scope before treating a spike as representative

## Troubleshooting
- **Tab empty or limited** — platform toggles are deployment-specific; ask your operator.
- **Do not treat as census** — social samples are indicative; prefer multi-source proof on the daily report.
