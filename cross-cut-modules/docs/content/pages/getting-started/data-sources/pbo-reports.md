## What it is
**Population-behavior officer (PBO)** reports — municipality files and **regional daily summaries** from the field.

## In the app
Open **Data sources** → **PBO reports**. Browse files and summaries that fed today's run.

## When to use it
- Verify a locality-specific score against officer-submitted context
- Compare regional summaries when National and North views diverge

## Troubleshooting
- **Missing municipality** — not every locality files every day; thin evidence on the report reflects gaps.
- **Old dates** — check the run date selector; PBO uploads may lag the news cycle.
