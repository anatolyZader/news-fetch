## Executive Summary

On March 21, the behavioral landscape is defined by a durable split between structural functioning and lived experience. Institutional systems — leadership communication, community resource mobilization, and shelter compliance — continue to operate and in some domains show improvement over prior days. Mayors are issuing explicit public positions on school and camp closures, formal compensation frameworks are being negotiated and announced, volunteers are distributing food baskets, and residents are entering shelters repeatedly and consistently across wide geographic areas. The Belzer Rebbe's letter reinforcing shelter compliance and the Home Front Command chief's in-person visit to Kiryat Shmona are examples of leadership actively maintaining behavioral norms and visible presence at the community level.
