## Who this is for
You open Srulik's lab to **read today's homefront assessment** — not to run code or configure servers. This page tells you where to click and what each area shows.

## Open the app
1. Open the link your organization gave you.
2. Sign in if asked (usually with an approved Google account).
3. You land on **Daily assessment** — today's main report.

## Read the daily report (2 minutes)
The report summarizes community resilience in **eight components** (shelter behavior, cohesion, services, and similar themes).

**Quick pass**
- Read the top summary.
- Note which components moved most since yesterday.

**When something surprises you**
- Open that component's **evidence** list.
- Check whether the sources support the score.

Use **National** / **North** at the top if your team works on the northern scope.

## Ask questions (chat)
Click the chat button (bottom-right). Ask specific questions, for example:
- "Why did community cohesion drop?"
- "What evidence supports the highest-risk component?"

Answers use evidence already in the report.

## Send your own input
**Send data** (top bar) — paste a link, quote, or short observation. It is reviewed and can appear in the next daily run.

**Write report** — for staff who file structured manual reports (not needed for most readers).

## Data sources (input feeds)
These tabs show the **input feeds** behind the assessment — not the report itself. Start with [Data sources (overview)](data-sources.md), then open any feed below.

The **Daily assessment** view is the synthesized report (see [Read the daily report](#read-the-daily-report-2-minutes) above). Return to it via the header title or back control above the tabs.

- [News](data-sources/news.md) — homefront-filtered articles
- [WhatsApp](data-sources/whatsapp.md) — approved groups and structured reports
- [Audio and radio](data-sources/audio-radio.md) — transcribed broadcasts
- [PBO reports](data-sources/pbo-reports.md) — field officer files
- [Report bot](data-sources/report-bot.md) — structured situational submissions
- [Visits](data-sources/visits.md) — squad field observations
- [Social media](data-sources/social-media.md) — public posts and topic searches
- [Pools](data-sources/pools.md) — Naftali and Education questionnaires
- [Google Trends](data-sources/google-trends.md) — search-interest early warning
- [Send data](data-sources/send-data.md) — your links and observations (top bar)

## Settings & docs
**⋮ menu** (top-right) → **Settings** (language, email digests if enabled, privacy) or **Documentation** (full help library).

Footer links open the same docs and legal pages without using the menu.

## Troubleshooting
- **No report / old date** — today's run may not have finished; ask your operator or refresh later.
- **Score seems off** — open the evidence under that component first; thin evidence means low confidence.
- **Can't sign in** — your account may not be authorized; contact your operator.

For more detail, see [Using the app (first report)](using-the-app.md).
